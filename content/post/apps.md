These are small, free web apps I've built. They run entirely in your browser: there's nothing to install, no account to create and no ads. Your progress and settings are saved in your browser, not on a server. More apps are on the way.

## [Words](/words/)

Learn the most common words in English, French, Spanish or German in just a few minutes a day. Words is a flashcard app with spaced repetition: each day it mixes a few new words with the ones you're due to review, and it explains them in the language of your choice. It works offline and can be installed on your phone like a native app.

## [Bookshelf](/bookshelf/)

Turn a photo of a bookshelf into an interactive catalogue. Upload a picture and every spine becomes a hotspot: hover over it to see the title, or click for details and places to find the book. Scanning uses Anthropic's Claude with your own API key, and you can try it first on an example shelf without one.
