The Artist and the Scientist  
by Marc Cohen

<!--more-->  

What do you see, my dearest love  
When regarding the night time sky?  
Demanded the curious artist  
Of her clever science guy  

I see a glowing rock, said he,  
That rules the ocean's tide.  
I see a trillion suns,  
With only gravity their guide.  

I see a sea of cosmic dust,  
After eons of decay.  
I see the remnants of a bang  
Still echoing to this day.  

He turned to gaze upon his love,  
Continuing this game.  
And what do you see, he queried her,  
When you look upon the same?  

I see a loving friend, she replied
Illuminating this eve.  
I see a million points of light  
Reminding us to breathe.  

I see a blanket made of stars   
Comforting this night.  
I feel the vastness of this world   
Ever bathed in light.
