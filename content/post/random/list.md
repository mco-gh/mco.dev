The real luxuries in life:

- Time
- Health
- A quiet mind
- Slow mornings
- Ability to travel
- Rest without guilt
- A good night’s sleep
- Calm and “boring” days
- Meaningful conversations
- Home-cooked meals
- People you love
- People who love you back
