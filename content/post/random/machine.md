I'm a machine. So are you. We don't tend to think of ourselves that way, but there's a good reason why we should.

<!--more-->

We tend to think of a "machine" as something designed and built by a human. That's conventional because until recently, the vast majority of machines were built by people. But that's changing fast.

Let's look at the definition:

> A machine is a mechanical, electrical, or electronic device that uses power to apply forces, control movement, and perform specific work or tasks.

You could argue that we don't fit the "mechanical, electrical, or electronic" classification but actually we are hybrids of all of those functional models. Certainly our musculo-skeletal system is mechanical, and our nervous system operates by small pulses of biochemical impulses.
