Yesterday I happened to see an ad for KFC featuring an older gentleman playing a heartfelt cover of *Linger* by the Cranberries. A quick Google search revealed the name of the musician so, of course, I had to learn everything I could about <a target="_blank" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Watkinson">Frank Watkinson</a>.

<!--more-->

Frank is a a retired, 73 years old Brit, who's been recording acoustic covers and original songs since 2011 on his <a target="_blank" href="https://www.youtube.com/@Virtualgranddad">Youtube channel</a>, which now has over one million subscribers.
