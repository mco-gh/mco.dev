What we're about

Edinburgh Butch Club is a social group for butches, studs, mascs, and the people who love them. We put on nights out, meetups, and collaborations with other queer groups across the city.

Everything we run is alcohol-free and planned with access in mind. That way more of us can get in the room and enjoy it.

  • Sober by design. No pressure to drink, and events built around something other than the bar.
  • Accessibility first. We share venue access info up front and ask for your feedback after every event.
  • Butch, stud, masc, and friends. However you name your gender or presentation, you're welcome here.
  • Solidarity. Proudly queer, trans-inclusive, and standing with Palestine.

Accessibility

We want our events to work for as many people as possible. For each event we aim to share the venue's step-free access, toilets, seating, and noise and lighting levels ahead of time.

If something would make an event easier for you, get in touch before the event and we'll do what we can.
