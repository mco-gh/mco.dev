    About BG

    BG is backgammon for two people, wherever they are. There are no accounts, no ads and no saved games: just a board, two dice and a friend.

    Starting a game

    1. Pick your color and choose Start a game.
    2. Send the code (or the link) to your friend.
    3. When they join, the opening roll decides who goes first.

    Playing

    • Your home is the bottom right. Move your checkers around the board towards it, then bear them off into the tray.
    • Roll, then tap a glowing checker and tap where it should land. You can chain both dice in one tap.
    • Land on a single opposing checker to send it to the bar. A point with two or more is closed to you.
    • You must use as many dice as you can. Undo takes a move back; Done ends your turn.
    • The first to bear off all fifteen checkers wins.

    Video and chat

    Turn on your camera or microphone at any time during a game, or just type. Each of you decides separately what to share.

    Privacy

    BG is a static page. A public PeerJS server introduces your two browsers using the game code; after that, moves, chat, video and audio travel directly between you over WebRTC. Nothing is recorded, and only your color settings are saved, in this browser.

    Leave this game?

    The game ends for both of you and can’t be resumed.
