      About Chess

      Chess for two people, wherever they are. There are no accounts, no ads, no clocks and no saved games: just a board and a friend.

      Starting a game

      1. Pick white, black or a surprise, then choose Start a game.
      2. Send the code (or the link) to your friend.
      3. When they join, white moves first.

      Playing

      • Tap a piece to see where it can go, then tap a marked square. Or just drag it there.
      • Only legal moves are offered, including castling (move the king two squares) and capturing en passant.
      • A pawn that reaches the far side becomes the piece you choose.
      • The game ends by checkmate or resignation, or in a draw by stalemate, agreement, the same position three times, fifty moves with no capture or pawn move, or too few pieces left to mate.
      • Play again starts a rematch with the colors swapped.

      Video and chat

      Turn on your camera or microphone at any time during a game, or just type. Each of you decides separately what to share.

      Privacy

      Chess is a static page. A public PeerJS server introduces your two browsers using the game code; after that, moves, chat, video and audio travel directly between you over WebRTC. Nothing is recorded, and only your color settings are saved, in this browser.

      Resign this game?

      Your opponent wins. You can play again afterwards.

      Leave this game?

      The game ends for both of you and can’t be resumed.

      Connection lost

      The link to your opponent dropped.
